I am a Computer Science PhD student at the University of California Santa Cruz, and I am advised by [Daniel Fremont](https://people.ucsc.edu/~dfremont/index.html). My research is centered in the area of Formal Methods, specifically the development and applications of the [Scenic](https://scenic-lang.org/) probabilistic programming language.

During my PhD I have had the opportunity to intern at [Nissan Advanced Technology Center](https://www.nissan-global.com/EN/INNOVATION/TECHNOLOGY/ADVANCED_TECH_CENTER/) and [Toyota Research Institute North America](https://amrd.toyota.com/division/trina/), applying my academic research towards real-world autonomous vehicle problems.

As an undergraduate, I worked under [Katia Obraczka](https://inrg.soe.ucsc.edu/) on the [PIMAP](https://inrg.soe.ucsc.edu/project-pimap-pressure-injury-monitoring-and-prevention/) project.
